God With Us Then & Now

Emmanuel means "God with us" – and that's the heart of everything we do. We believe God is actively present in our worship, our community, and our daily lives.

Founded over a decade ago, Dominion Global Church On Fire has grown from a small home fellowship to a thriving community of believers. Yet our mission remains the same: to share the love of Jesus Christ with everyone we meet.

We offer vibrant worship, practical teaching, and genuine relationships. No matter where you are in your journey, there's a place for you at Dominion Global Church On Fire.
